How this page works

A KAPEVault vault is one JSON file. Its header names the format (kapevault, or bovault from before the rename), version 1, and the scrypt settings with a random 16-byte salt. Your master password goes through scrypt (N up to 2^17, r = 8, p = 1) to make a 256-bit key, and AES-256-GCM with a 12-byte IV and a 16-byte tag seals the whole vault. This page repeats exactly that with your browser's own WebCrypto and a built-in scrypt. Its security policy blocks every network request, and nothing is saved, so closing the tab forgets everything.
